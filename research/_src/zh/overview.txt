Grok Bot is your proactive AI teammate. It can help you build a deck, run a marketing campaign, book a dinner reservation, or keep your calendar in sync with all of your schedule changes.
It's an agent with access to a real computer in the cloud, so it can do all the things you can.

Grok Bot for teams 

An entire team of bots 
Bots can take on big tasks that might otherwise take hours or days. They work across apps, websites, and tools, and with one another: multiple Bots can work together, like a real team.
Chat is the main interface for Grok Bot, but you can also start a voice call or send a voice note. Bots work best when you connect them with the tools you use every day: Slack, Notion, Drive, Teams, and more. Grok Bot supports custom tooling, so it can interact with any MCP or API.
